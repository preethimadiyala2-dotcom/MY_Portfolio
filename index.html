[ Project Overview ]

Performance Optimization of CMOS Inverter is a machine-learning project built to replace slow, repeated simulations. By combining LTspice simulation, feature engineering, and regression models, it turns circuit trade-offs into fast, confident design decisions.

Problem

Finding the right transistor sizes for a CMOS inverter takes many SPICE runs, each measuring delay, power and energy one at a time.

Goal

To build a model that predicts propagation delay, average power and energy per switching directly from design parameters.

Solution

A ~300-row LTspice dataset with engineered features (Wp/Wn, CV product, W/L), used to train Random Forest and ExtraTrees regressors.

Outcome

R² above 0.9 on test data, fast predictions in place of repeated SPICE runs, and design points that balance speed and power.
